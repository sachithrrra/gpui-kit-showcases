# Torrie

A fast, simple BitTorrent client for macOS, with a native UI, plugins and a remote control API.

## Features

- **Add anything**: drag in torrent files, click magnet links, or paste one
- **Choose files** before and during a download, with per-file progress
- **Inspector**: overview, files, peers with country flags, and trackers
- **Speed limits** with an alternative set you can schedule by time and weekday
- **Watch while downloading**: play video and audio in IINA, VLC or your default player
- **Menu bar item** with live speeds, and opening at login
- **Plugins**: add menu commands, Inspector tabs and settings, or run actions when a download finishes
- **Web remote**: manage downloads from any browser on your phone or another computer
- **Remote control API** for your own apps and scripts
- **Self-updating**, installing only notarized builds signed by the same developer
- DHT, UPnP, stop seeding at a ratio, fast resume, and light and dark mode

Learn more at https://torrie.download or browse the source at https://github.com/kronzhq/torrie.
